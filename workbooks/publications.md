# SARGEN™ — WORKBOOK: PUBLICATIONS

**Status:** Working Record  
**Scope:** Publication-level working records and the distinct contribution each publication makes to the SARGEN™ body of work.

## Purpose

This workbook organizes publication notes and contribution records by publication. It complements—but does not replace—the canonical [SARGEN™ Publication Register](../publications/SARGEN%E2%84%A2%20Publication%20Register).

The Publication Register remains the source for official publication metadata. This workbook captures the publication's approved context, central distinction, and cross-workbook coordination notes where useful.

## Record Rules

- Do not assume the article sequence number from the Publication ID.
- Preserve the exact approved title, story preview, publication date, and public link.
- Distinguish published facts from interpretation and proposed future work.
- Add new entries without rewriting prior approved entries unless explicitly requested.
